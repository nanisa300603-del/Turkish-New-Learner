The Ottoman Empire: Confluence of Three Continents

Founded by Osman I in 1299, the Ottoman Empire grew into one of the most powerful empires in human history. Under Sultan Mehmed II (Fatih Sultan Mehmet), Constantinople was conquered in 1453, becoming Istanbul—the magnificent capital bridging Europe and Asia.

Key Milestone: 1453 Conquest of Istanbul marked the end of the Middle Ages. The empire tolerated diverse linguistic and religious groups under the Millet system.

🇹🇷 Cumhuriyetimizin Kurucusu

Mustafa Kemal Atatürk & The Modern Turkish Republic

In 1923, following the Turkish War of Independence, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk founded the modern, secular Republic of Turkey. He spearheaded radical reforms: the Latin-based Turkish alphabet (1928), equal rights and suffrage for women, universal education, and modern statecraft.

Famous Motto: "Yurtta barış, dünyada barış" ("Peace at home, peace in the world"). Atatürk personally traveled across the country with a portable blackboard to teach the new alphabet.
